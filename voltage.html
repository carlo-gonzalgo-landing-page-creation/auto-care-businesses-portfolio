    Questions

    Quick answers.

    Don’t see yours? Send us a message below.

    How long does installation take?

    A partial front is usually done in a day. A whole car can take several days, including prep and a cure check.

    Will the film be visible?

    Quality film is optically clear. Look closely at the edges and you may see a thin line, which is normal.

    How do I wash it afterward?

    Wait about a week, then hand wash. Avoid pressure-washing near film edges.

    Can I put ceramic coating on top?

    Yes. A coating on top of film makes it easier to wash and adds extra water beading.

    Does it work on matte paint?

    Matte-painted cars need matte film. Tell us about your finish when you request a quote.
