    Learn the truths about paint protection film

    01

    Myth: film is only for luxury cars

    Daily drivers and family SUVs get chipped on the same roads. Film suits any car that you want to keep looking new.

    02

    Myth: film will peel or bubble

    Peeling usually comes from poor prep and installation. We wash, clay and polish first, then heat-form every edge.

    03

    Myth: it can’t come off cleanly

    Film is made to be removed. Done properly, it peels away and the original paint is underneath.

    Your film questions, answered

    How long does an install take?

    Front packages take one to two days. A full car is usually four to five, including cure checks.

    When can I wash the car?

    Wait about a week, then hand wash. Keep pressure washers away from film edges.

    Can I add a ceramic coating on top?

    Yes, after the film has cured. Ask us about the ceramic top-coat in the full body package.

    Does it hold up in Philippine weather?

    Film adds a replaceable layer against sun, rain, sap and road grit. Ask for written warranty terms before you pay.
