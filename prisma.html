Straight answers

Questions we get every week.

How long does it take?

A front package is typically 1–2 days; a full car is usually 4–5 days including cure checks. You get a date when we quote.

Can I wash it after?

Wait about a week, then hand wash. Avoid pressure-washing film edges. We hand you a printed care sheet.

Will the film yellow?

Quality TPU films are rated against yellowing, and the warranty on this sample page is a placeholder. Ask for the written warranty before you pay.

Can it come off later?

Yes. Film peels off the factory paint and leaves the original finish underneath, which helps resale.

Do you do matte or satin?

Satin film is available. Matte-painted cars need a matte film, so ask us before booking.
