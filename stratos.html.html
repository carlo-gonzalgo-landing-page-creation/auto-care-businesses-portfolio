Straight answers

Before you ask.

How long does it take?

Front packages take 1–2 days. A full car is usually 4–5 days including cure checks.

When can I wash it?

Wait about a week, then hand wash. Skip pressure washers near film edges.

Can I remove it later?

Yes. Film peels off and leaves the original paint underneath.

Is it worth it in Cebu?

Salt air, strong sun and long highway runs all wear on paint. Film adds a replaceable layer on top. Ask for the written warranty before you pay.
